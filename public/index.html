WhatsApp Chat to PDF

Turn an exported chat (with photos, voice notes, videos and documents) into a PDF that looks like WhatsApp

Drop the exported chat ZIP here
or click to choose a file (.zip, or a .txt without media)
How to export a chat: open the chat in WhatsApp › tap the contact or group name (or ⋮ › More) › Export chat › Include media, then save or send yourself the ZIP file.
Your chat is read in your browser. Only the finished page is sent to this site's server to be printed as a PDF, and it is not stored.

Your messages appear in green on the right.
